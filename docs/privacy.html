Privacy Policy

Last updated October 8, 2026

The short version

  • Help Senpai has no accounts, no servers of its own and no analytics.
  • Your notes, context, history, settings and API keys are stored only on your computer.
  • When you use a cloud AI provider, the audio, screenshots and text needed for an answer go directly from your computer to the provider you chose, using your own API key.
  • In Local AI mode, answers and transcription run on your computer and nothing is sent to an AI provider.

Who this policy covers

This policy describes how the Help Senpai desktop app ("the app") and this website (help-senpai.vercel.app) handle information. Help Senpai is an open-source project, so you can check everything here against the source code.

Information stored on your computer

The app saves the following in a senpai-config folder in your user profile (for example %APPDATA%\senpai-config on Windows):

  • Settings and preferences, such as the provider and models you picked, your language and your shortcuts.
  • API keys you enter for OpenRouter, Google Gemini or Groq. They are stored in a plain JSON file on your device and are only sent to the provider they belong to.
  • Notes you write in the Notes page.
  • Instructions you type on the Context page, such as the role, company and interview format.
  • Context you add: resume or portfolio text, assignment details, and text pulled from GitHub repositories, web pages, PDF and DOCX files you choose.
  • Session history: transcripts and answers from past sessions.

We cannot see or access this folder. You can delete individual sessions and notes in the app, erase everything with Settings → Clear all data, or delete the folder yourself. Uninstalling the app does not delete this folder, so your notes survive reinstalls and updates.

Information sent to AI providers

To produce an answer, the app sends a request directly from your computer to the provider you selected: OpenRouter, Google (Gemini API) or Groq. Depending on what you do, a request can contain:

  • short recordings of speech picked up from system audio and, if enabled, your microphone, for transcription;
  • screenshots, only when you request one (for example with Ctrl + Enter);
  • messages you type and the recent conversation;
  • your custom instructions, and the context and notes you marked to share with the AI.

Each provider handles that data under its own privacy policy and terms, which may include logging, retention, or use for improving its services, particularly on free tiers. OpenRouter forwards requests to the model's underlying provider. Please read the policies of the providers you use and adjust their settings (for example data-sharing or training options) to suit you. Help Senpai does not receive a copy.

In Local AI mode, transcription and answers run on your computer. The app only goes online to download the model files you choose.

Other network requests the app makes

  • Context you add: when you add a GitHub repository or web page, the app downloads it from GitHub or that website.
  • Model lists and key checks: the app asks your provider which models are available and whether your key works.
  • Local model downloads: Local AI models are downloaded from Hugging Face when you choose them.
  • Update checks: the Windows app checks for new versions through update.electronjs.org, a service run by the Electron project, and downloads updates from GitHub Releases. These requests include the app's version and your operating system, and, like any web request, your IP address. They include no personal content.

This website

This website has no cookies, no analytics and no sign-up. It is hosted by Vercel, which may keep standard server logs such as IP address and browser type. Fonts load from Google Fonts, the star count loads from the GitHub API, and downloads come from GitHub, so those services see ordinary request information. Your light or dark theme choice is saved in your browser's local storage and never leaves your device.

Recording other people

The app can transcribe audio that includes other people. Many places require you to tell people, or get their consent, before you record or transcribe a conversation. You are responsible for following those laws and the rules of any meeting, interview, class or exam. See our Terms.

Children

Help Senpai is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Security

Data in senpai-config is protected by your computer's user account, not by extra encryption. Anyone who can sign in to your account can read it, so keep your device locked and your API keys private. If you think a key has leaked, revoke it in your provider's dashboard.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top. The history of this page is public in the repository.

Contact

Questions or concerns? Open an issue on GitHub.
